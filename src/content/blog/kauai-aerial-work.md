I'm on **Kauai** for aerial work — the Garden Isle from above, with the drone, and a stack of new stills fresh out of the gallery.

If you've been following Aeromatic from Bend and the West Coast, nothing about the craft changes: clean framing, careful ops, and deliverables clients can use right away. What changes is the backdrop — ridgelines into cloud, emerald valleys, and shoreline light you only get out here.

The video at the top of this post is a new upload from these flights. Below are stills from the same recent sessions — coastal passes, inland texture, and a few frames that only work when the weather cooperates.
